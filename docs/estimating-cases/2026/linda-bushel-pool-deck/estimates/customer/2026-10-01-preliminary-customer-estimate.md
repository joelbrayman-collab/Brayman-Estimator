## Scope described by the approved brief

- Above-ground pool about 21 ft in diameter and about 50 in high. Field verify.
- Upper deck 18 ft wide, pool edge curved and left open, other three edges straight, 5 ft clear at the centreline.
- Stairs 10 ft wide, about 38 in rise, five equal risers, about 11 in treads.
- Lower deck 10 ft by 3 ft.
- Centred gate, 42 in clear, outward swinging, self-closing and self-latching.
- Freestanding. No structural attachment to the pool.
- 10 in sonotube piers, 48 in below grade, subject to bearing and the municipality.
- 6×6 posts, double 2×10 beams, 2×8 joists at 16 in on centre maximum, segmented curved rim.
- 5/4×6 decking, 2×12 stringers, Veranda rail appearance, 4×4 guard posts, 1×6 skirt on the lower deck.

## Assumptions

- Option A, the straight main beam with wing beams, is the framing basis named in the design brief.
- Brown pressure-treated lumber.
- The upper deck is the top stair landing, so the preliminary stair uses four treads.
- Two 5/4×6 boards are the specified tread. A later stair proof draws a wider overall tread. Extra tread stock was not added.
- Guard product named in the brief is the 37 in Veranda kit. A separate note of about 42 in, and the brief's own estimate of about 49 in from grade to the lower rail, were not averaged.
- Waste on the take-off is the offcut from the stated stock. No extra percentage is in the customer scope.
