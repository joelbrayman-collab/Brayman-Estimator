# Linda Bushel Pool Deck - Construction Drawing and Supplier Package Design

Date: September 29, 2026  
Prepared for: Brayman Construction Inc.  
Project location: D'Arcy's Way, Kemptville, ON K0G 1J0

## 1. Purpose

Convert Linda Bushel's client sketch and the approved preliminary geometry into a coordinated 11 x 17 construction-drawing basis, material take-off, and supplier quotation package for Darcy at BMR Winchester.

The package is intended to support client confirmation, municipal permit review, Brayman construction planning, and supplier pricing. It is not a professional-engineering seal or municipal approval.

## 2. Approved Geometry

- Existing above-ground pool: 21 ft diameter and approximately 50 in. high.
- Upper deck: 18 ft overall width.
- Pool-facing deck edge follows the pool curve; the remaining three deck edges are straight.
- Clear upper-deck depth at the centreline: 5 ft from the pool edge to the top of the stairs.
- Stair: 10 ft overall width, centred on the upper and lower decks.
- Lower deck: 10 ft wide x 3 ft deep and approximately 12 in. above grade.
- Gate: centred on the lower-deck entrance, 42 in. clear opening.
- Stair elevation basis: approximately 38 in. between deck surfaces, using five equal field-adjusted risers at approximately 7.6 in. and approximately 11 in. treads.

Actual pool dimensions, installed elevation, finished grades, setbacks and property constraints must be field verified before permit submission or construction.

## 3. Structural Design Basis

- Freestanding construction with no structural attachment to the pool.
- Framing layout: approved Option A - one straight main beam with short supported wing beams serving the deeper portions beside the curved pool edge.
- Foundations: 10 in. diameter concrete sonotube piers extending 48 in. below finished grade, subject to field bearing and municipal confirmation.
- Structural posts: brown pressure-treated 6 x 6 posts on galvanized adjustable post saddles.
- Beams: built-up double brown pressure-treated 2 x 10 members.
- Joists: brown pressure-treated 2 x 8 members at 16 in. on centre.
- Curved pool-side edge: segmented pressure-treated rim/header framing following the verified pool radius while maintaining structural separation from the pool.
- Decking: brown pressure-treated 5/4 x 6 deck boards running parallel to the 18 ft front edge.
- Stair stringers: brown pressure-treated 2 x 12 stringers at not more than 16 in. on centre.
- Stair treads: two brown pressure-treated 5/4 x 6 boards per tread.
- Skirting: vertical brown pressure-treated 1 x 6 boards installed tight, allowing natural shrinkage to establish small drainage and ventilation gaps.
- Structural connectors and fasteners: exterior-rated, corrosion-resistant and compatible with pressure-treated lumber.

Final spans, pier locations, connector schedules and member cuts will be reconciled within the drawing and take-off model before issue.

## 4. Guard, Stair and Pool-Enclosure Basis

- Primary guard system: Veranda 37 in. x 6 ft brown pressure-treated rail kit with black aluminum balusters, Home Depot model HDDR2022005 / SKU 1001900458.
- Guard posts: brown pressure-treated 4 x 4 posts with code-appropriate structural blocking and connections.
- Guards are required along every exposed upper-deck edge, both outside stair edges and the exposed lower-deck perimeter.
- No centre stair handrail.
- Curved pool-facing deck edge remains open for pool access.
- Gate: 42 in. clear, centred on the lower deck, outward swinging, self-closing and self-latching, with visual treatment matching the selected rail kit.
- Custom stair guard and gate components shall match the pressure-treated wood and black aluminum-baluster appearance.
- Lower-deck skirting closes the area below the guard and gate.
- The estimated combined lower-deck and rail height is approximately 49 in. above grade; field verification and municipal acceptance are required for the 1.2 m pool-enclosure requirement.

The Brayman-branded drawing set will be issued as an 11 x 17 landscape PDF and include:

1. Cover/general notes and approved geometry.
2. Dimensioned pier/foundation plan.
3. Post, beam, wing-beam, joist, blocking and curved-header framing plan.
4. Decking, stair, guard, gate and skirting plan.
5. Front and side elevations.
6. Stair riser/tread and stringer details.
7. Typical pier, saddle, post, beam, joist, guard-post and connector details.
8. Field-verification, permit and supplier notes.

Each sheet will show the Brayman Construction logo, 411 St. John Street, Merrickville, ON K0G 1N0, Linda Bushel's name, the D'Arcy's Way project location, date, revision and sheet number.

## 7. Material Take-Off

The take-off will calculate and reconcile:

- concrete and 10 in. sonotubes;
- 6 x 6 structural posts and adjustable saddles;
- 2 x 10 beam stock and beam fasteners;
- 2 x 8 joists, rim/header stock and blocking;
- 2 x 12 stair stringers;
- 5/4 x 6 decking and stair-tread boards;
- 1 x 6 skirting;
- Veranda rail kits and 4 x 4 guard posts;
- matching gate materials and self-closing/self-latching hardware;
- joist hangers, angles, post/beam connectors, bolts, structural screws, deck screws and compatible miscellaneous hardware;
- concrete, lumber and trim waste allowances shown explicitly by category.

Quantities must reconcile to the drawing geometry. Stock lengths, cut allocation and useful offcuts must be shown so the supplier can quote the intended purchasing quantities.

## 8. BMR Winchester Supplier Package

The supplier package for Darcy will include:

- project and delivery address;
- grouped product descriptions and preferred stock lengths;
- required quantities and units;
- BMR product/SKU mapping when a reliable public match exists;
- public website price as an indicative reference only;
- separate fields for BMR Winchester contractor price, availability, substitution and delivery timing;
- unresolved supplier confirmations clearly flagged.

Public BMR website pricing must not be treated as Brayman's final cost. Darcy's returned quotation is the governing supplier evidence.

## 9. Verification and Release Controls

- verify stair rise/run consistency and guard continuity;
- verify the 42 in. gate and lower-deck enclosure geometry;
- confirm the selected Veranda product dimensions and panel allocation;
- distinguish confirmed dimensions from field or municipal verification items;
- visually inspect every 11 x 17 sheet for clipping, overlaps and legibility;
- independently recalculate concrete, board-foot/lineal-foot, panel and hardware totals.

The first issue will be marked **Preliminary Construction and Supplier Pricing Basis - Subject to Permit Review and Field Verification**.
