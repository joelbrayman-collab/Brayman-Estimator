# Connect your calendar (Outlook, Google, Apple)

Your CalibraytAI appointments can show in the calendar you already use, and update by themselves.

**What it is:** a one-way, read-only feed. You make and change appointments in CalibraytAI; your phone's
calendar shows them. Nothing you type in Outlook, Google or Apple Calendar comes back into CalibraytAI,
and CalibraytAI never needs your Google, Microsoft or Apple password.

**What is in it:** your appointments for the 60 days behind and 400 days ahead: the ones for you, the ones
you attend, the ones you made for no one in particular, and your personal ones. Not other people's personal
appointments. The crew's job schedule is not in the feed: crew see their work on the phone in
Field → Week and Month.

## Turn it on (once)

1. Calendar → **Calendar connection** → **Turn on my calendar feed**.
2. Your private address appears **once**. Copy it now (Copy button). Anyone with the address can read
   your appointments, so keep it to yourself.

## iPhone (Apple Calendar)

Easiest: open Calendar connection **on the iPhone in Safari**, turn the feed on (or make a new address),
tap **Open in Apple Calendar** → **Subscribe** → **Add**.

Or by hand: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other →
**Add Subscribed Calendar** → paste the address → Next → Save.
(Before iOS 18: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar.)

## Mac (Apple Calendar)

Calendar → File → **New Calendar Subscription** → paste the address → Subscribe. Set **Auto-refresh**
to every 5 or 15 minutes → OK.

## Google Calendar

Google only adds a calendar by address on a computer (it then shows on the phone too):

1. calendar.google.com → left side, **Other calendars** → **+** → **From URL**.
2. Paste the address → **Add calendar**.
3. On an Android phone or the Google Calendar app, it appears under the account a little later.

## Outlook

- Outlook on the web / new Outlook: Calendar → **Add calendar** → **Subscribe from web** → paste the
  address → name it CalibraytAI → **Import**.
- Classic Outlook for Windows: Calendar → Add Calendar → **From Internet** → paste → OK → Yes.

## How fast changes show

- Apple Calendar: as often as you set (5 minutes or more).
- Google and Outlook: they decide; usually within a few hours, sometimes up to a day. This is their
  setting, not CalibraytAI's.
- A moved appointment moves (it is the same event, never a copy). A cancelled appointment disappears.

## Lost phone, or someone left

- Calendar connection → **Make a new address**: the old one stops at once; add the new one to your
  calendars.
- **Turn off**: the feed stops.
- When a person is deactivated in People & Access, their feed stops at once.

## One appointment only

On any appointment, **Add to my calendar** downloads that one appointment (a .ics file) for any calendar.
It does not update afterwards; use the feed for that.
