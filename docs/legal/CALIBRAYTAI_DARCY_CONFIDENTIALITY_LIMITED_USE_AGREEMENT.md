## Agreement text

CALIBRAYTAI  
CONFIDENTIALITY AND LIMITED USE AGREEMENT

This Confidentiality and Limited Use Agreement (the “Agreement”) is between Brayman & Associates Inc., on behalf of CalibraytAI (“Brayman”) and Darcy __________________ (“Darcy”), effective __________________, 2026.

### 1. PURPOSE

Brayman would like to give Darcy an opportunity to see and evaluate CalibraytAI and to discuss how the platform could potentially work with contractors, suppliers and other participants in the construction industry.

The purpose of this Agreement is to allow those discussions to take place openly while protecting both the non-public information Brayman shares and the CalibraytAI platform and development work Darcy will be given an opportunity to see.

### 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

“Confidential Information” means non-public information disclosed or made available to Darcy in connection with CalibraytAI, whether verbally, visually, electronically or in writing.

It includes non-public technical, business and commercial information, software, documentation, designs, development plans, pricing information, data, methods and other proprietary information relating to CalibraytAI.

Confidential Information does not include information Darcy can demonstrate was already lawfully known to him, becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement, or is independently developed without using or referring to information received from Brayman.

### 3. CONFIDENTIALITY

Darcy agrees to keep Brayman's Confidential Information confidential and not disclose it to another person without Brayman's prior consent.

Darcy will use reasonable care to protect that information from unauthorized disclosure.

### 4. LIMITED PURPOSE OF ACCESS

Darcy is being given access to CalibraytAI solely so that he can evaluate the platform and discuss a possible business relationship with Brayman.

That access does not give Darcy any ownership, licence or right to commercially use or reproduce CalibraytAI.

### 5. NO REPLICATION OR COMPETITIVE USE

Darcy agrees that he will not use his access to CalibraytAI, or the information, functionality, workflows, architecture, methods, designs, processes or know-how demonstrated or disclosed to him through that access, as a basis or blueprint to copy, recreate, reverse engineer or replicate CalibraytAI, or to develop or assist another person in developing a competing or substantially similar platform derived from CalibraytAI.

This restriction applies to the use of what Darcy learns through his access to CalibraytAI and does not depend upon every individual feature, concept or idea demonstrated being independently confidential or proprietary.

Nothing in this Agreement prevents Darcy from continuing his existing business, working with other technology providers, or independently developing or using products, systems or ideas that are developed without using CalibraytAI or information obtained through his access to it.

### 6. OWNERSHIP

Nothing in this Agreement transfers to Darcy any ownership interest in CalibraytAI or any intellectual property owned by Brayman or its applicable licensors.

### 7. DEMONSTRATIONS AND MATERIALS

Unless Brayman agrees otherwise, Darcy will not record a CalibraytAI demonstration or distribute screenshots, documentation or other non-public materials provided by Brayman.

### 8. TERM

The confidentiality obligations under this Agreement will continue for 18 months from the date of this Agreement.

Information qualifying as a trade secret will remain protected for so long as it continues to qualify for protection under applicable law.

The expiry of the confidentiality period does not give Darcy a right to use materials or information obtained through his access to CalibraytAI in violation of any intellectual property or other rights that continue to exist under applicable law.

### 9. REQUIRED DISCLOSURE

If Darcy is legally required to disclose Confidential Information, he will, where legally permitted, promptly notify Brayman and disclose only what is legally required.

### 10. REMEDIES

The parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure or misuse may cause harm that is difficult to remedy solely with monetary damages. Brayman may therefore seek appropriate injunctive or other remedies available at law in the event of a breach.

### 11. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

Neither party is required to enter into any further agreement or business relationship. This Agreement simply allows Darcy and Brayman to explore the potential opportunity openly.

### 12. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.

### 13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement represents the understanding between the parties concerning the confidentiality and permitted use of the information and access provided for this purpose. Any changes must be agreed to in writing by both parties.
