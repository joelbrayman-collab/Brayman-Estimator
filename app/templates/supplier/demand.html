How this is counted

  • Only requests contractors sent to {{ brand_for(supplier).name }}. Withdrawn requests are left out, and a request replaced by a newer one counts once.
  • Contractors are counted, never named here; no project, address or customer is shown, and nothing sent to any other supplier.
  • "You priced" counts the times you returned a SKU and price for the product. "Ordered from you" is what contractors ordered through CalibraytAI. Neither shows whether a contractor bought it elsewhere.
  • "No confirmed SKU" means CalibraytAI has no contractor-recorded match between this product and one of your SKUs yet.
