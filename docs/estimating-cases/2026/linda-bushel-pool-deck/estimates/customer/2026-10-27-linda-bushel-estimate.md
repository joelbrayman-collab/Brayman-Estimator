## Project

A new freestanding deck beside your above-ground pool: an upper deck at the pool, a full-width stair, and a small lower deck with a gate.

## Scope of work

**Foundation**

- Galvanized steel helical piles, screwed into the ground by a specialist foundation contractor, with adjustable steel brackets. No concrete footings.
- Pile size, number and locations to be confirmed with the foundation contractor before installation.

**Structure**

- Pressure-treated structural framing: built-up 2×10 beams, 2×8 joists, 6×6 posts and 2×12 stair stringers.
- Galvanized structural connectors, hangers, bolts and structural screws.
- The upper deck edge follows the curve of the pool. The deck stands on its own and is not attached to the pool.

**Decking and finish**

- Trex Select composite decking in **Island Mist** on the upper deck, the lower deck and the stair treads.
- Trex Island Mist fascia on the exposed edges, fastened with colour-matched screws.
- Trex deck fasteners.

**Upper deck, stairs and lower deck**

- Upper deck about 18 ft wide, with about 5 ft of clear deck at the centre of the pool.
- 10 ft wide stairs from the upper deck down to the lower deck.
- Lower deck 10 ft wide by 3 ft deep, with a pressure-treated 1×6 skirt around it.

**Railing and gate**

- Vista 42 in aluminium railing, textured black, standard pickets, on the upper deck sides and front, both sides of the stairs, and the lower deck.
- Matching 42 in high Vista gate, 48 in wide, centred on the lower deck.

**Labour**

- Supply and installation of everything listed above, by Brayman Construction.
- Site clean-up of construction debris on completion.

## Assumptions

- Pool about 21 ft in diameter and about 50 in high. All sizes are checked on site before materials are ordered.
- Clear access to the back yard for the pile-installation machine (about 46 in wide; a gate or fence panel may need to be removed and replaced).
- Underground services are located before the piles are installed. Any private buried lines (for example pool, power or irrigation) are pointed out by the owner.
- Normal soil conditions. If the foundation contractor finds the piles must go deeper to reach the required resistance, pile extensions are an additional cost, agreed with you first.
- Installation outside of freezing winter conditions, unless agreed otherwise.

## Not included

- Town building-permit fees, and an engineer's report or compliance letter if the town asks for one. Brayman can arrange both; their cost is confirmed before work starts.
- Changes to the pool, its equipment or its fence, other than reinstalling a removed panel.
- A guard along the open pool edge of the deck.
- Electrical work, lighting, landscaping, sod or grading.
- Removal of an existing deck or structure.
- Skirting under the upper deck.

## Permits and pool safety

The work follows the requirements of the town's building permit. Pool-enclosure rules (including fence and gate height and self-closing, self-latching gates) are confirmed with the town during the permit review. Any change the town requires is discussed with you before it is made.
