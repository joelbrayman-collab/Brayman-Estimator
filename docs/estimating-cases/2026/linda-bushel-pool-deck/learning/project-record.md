## Decisions recorded on the located files

The framing sheet marks Option A, a straight main beam with wing beams, as **Recommended**. The design brief calls that layout the approved framing basis. Options B and C are comparison alternatives on the same sheet. Option C is marked Not recommended because it conflicts with the pool.

The brief also records:

- Freestanding. No structural attachment to the pool.
- Brown pressure-treated lumber.
- 6×6 posts on galvanized adjustable saddles.
- Double 2×10 beams.
- 2×8 joists at 16 in. on centre.
- 2×12 stair stringers at not more than 16 in. on centre.
- 5/4×6 deck boards, parallel to the 18 ft front edge.
- Two 5/4×6 boards per stair tread.
- 10 in. sonotubes, 48 in. below finished grade, subject to field bearing and municipal confirmation.
- Veranda 37 in. × 6 ft brown pressure-treated rail kit, black aluminum balusters, Home Depot model HDDR2022005 / SKU 1001900458.
- No centre stair handrail.
- Curved pool-facing edge left open for pool access.
- Gate outward swinging, self-closing and self-latching.
- Skirt: vertical brown pressure-treated 1×6 boards installed tight. The brief says natural shrinkage may open small drainage gaps. It does not call for a deliberate ventilation gap.
- Brayman Construction Inc. address on the future sheets: 411 St. John Street, Merrickville, ON K0G 1N0.
- Drawing note required by the brief: prepared in accordance with the 2024 Ontario Building Code and applicable Municipality of North Grenville zoning, pool-enclosure, and permit requirements. Final construction subject to municipal review, field verification, and the issued permit.

That note is not a statement that North Grenville has approved this design. No permit approval is claimed.

## What this case may teach later

Only after a governed learning review, and only as evidence:

- A curved pool edge was kept off the main joist run by a straight beam plus short wing beams.
- A full rectangular frame was rejected on this sheet because it conflicts with the pool.
- Stair treads were specified as the same 5/4 deck board as the deck surface.
- Skirt boards were specified tight, without a designed ventilation slot.

Those points describe this job. They do not reset the work catalog, a calculation engine, or a cost default.
