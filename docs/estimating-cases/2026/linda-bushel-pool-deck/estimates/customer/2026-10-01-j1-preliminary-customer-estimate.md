## Project

Linda Bushel. Freestanding pressure-treated deck and stairs at an above-ground pool.

12 D'Arcy's Way, Kemptville, ON K0G 1J0.

The drawing in this package is a preliminary construction and pricing set. It is not a final construction drawing, not an engineering approval, not a town approval, and not a structural certification.

## Scope

- Above-ground pool about 21 ft in diameter and about 50 in high. Check on site.
- Upper deck 18 ft wide. The pool edge is curved and left open. The other three edges are straight. 5 ft clear at the centre.
- Framing: one straight main beam and two wing beams.
- 15 helical piers at the drawn locations. No sonotubes and no concrete. Shaft, helix, length, and capacity are not chosen.
- 16 joist lines. 2×8 joists. Curved header in straight pieces.
- Stairs 10 ft wide, about 38 in between the two decks, five risers about 7.6 in, four treads about 11 in. The upper deck is the top landing.
- 10 stair stringers. Each tread is two Trex boards.
- Stringer throat 5.00 in. That figure is a working size for this preliminary set. It is not an engineering certification.
- Lower deck 10 ft by 3 ft. Walking surface 12 in above grade.
- Guard: the named 37 in Veranda rail kit. Gate 42 in clear, swings out, self-closing and self-latching.
- Trex fascia on the upper side edges, the two upper front returns beside the stairs, and the lower deck sides and front. The open pool edge has no fascia. The stair stringer faces have no fascia. Colour, width, and stock length are not chosen.
- 1×6 skirt on the lower deck only, fitted tight.
- Freestanding. No attachment to the pool.

## Assumptions

- Framing is brown pressure-treated lumber. The walking surface, including the stair treads, is Trex composite. Colour and product line are not chosen.
- Joist spacing is 16 in on centre maximum, with a 4 in space at each upper outside edge.
- Stringer spacing uses the same 4 in edge spaces. Ten stringers.
- Waste is the leftover on the stated stock lengths. No extra percentage is added.
- The lower 6×6 posts are ordered uncut. Cut them on site. Beam, joist, and decking dress out at 17.5 in.
- Pool-enclosure compliance, including the 1.2 m rule, stays with the site and the town. The 37 in kit is not redrawn to another height.
