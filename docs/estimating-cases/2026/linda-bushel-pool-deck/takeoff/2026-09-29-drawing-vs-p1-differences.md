# Linda Bushel — drawing geometry against issue P1

Date: 2026-09-29

This is a difference report. It does not replace `2026-09-29-p1-material-takeoff.md`. No P1 quantity was changed.

CT-1 and CT-2 R2 are the proving drawings. Issue P1 is the preliminary quantity set. They do not share one layout. New sheets were not started from either layout alone.

## Stair rise and run

Both layouts use the same finished stair: upper surface 50 in, landing 12 in, total rise 38 in, five risers at 7.60 in, four treads at 11 in nosing-to-nosing, total horizontal run 44 in, stair width 10 ft.

That agreement is the rise and run. It is not a structural check of the cut stringer.

## Stringer throat

CT-2 R2 reports 4.997 in remaining throat, labeled 5.00 in, and **STRUCTURAL STRINGER VERIFICATION REQUIRED**.

No stringer-throat rule was found in the design brief, the take-off, or the drawing standard. Rise and run sitting inside the private-stair limits named on the sheet does not establish that the remaining 2×12 section is adequate.

Needed authority: a structural reviewer, or the prescriptive stair-stringer standard the municipality accepts for this cut 2×12, this rise, and this run. This report does not certify the throat.
