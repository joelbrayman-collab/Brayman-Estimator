THE CONTRACTOR–SUPPLIER CONNECTION

Supplier Connection

The contractor does the project work once. CalibraytAI carries it to the supplier the contractor chooses.

The contractor’s takeoff becomes a clear material request. In the Supplier Hub the supplier quotes it, receives the purchase order, confirms it and coordinates delivery, all connected to the same project and without rebuilding the takeoff.

THE WORK IS COMPLETED ONCE

The supplier receives the contractor’s front-end work—ready to price.

The contractor completes the scope, takeoff and quantities inside CalibraytAI and sends the material request to the supplier they choose. The supplier prices that request in the Supplier Hub instead of rebuilding the project.

Contractor work completed

The takeoff and quantities become one organized material request.

Sent by the contractor’s choice

The contractor decides which supplier receives which request. The supplier sees only what is sent.

Approval gates move it forward

Quotation, purchase order, confirmation and delivery each move forward when the right person approves them.

CONNECTED THROUGH APPROVAL GATES

Contractor work → Supplier quotation → Approval → Order → Delivery

CalibraytAI removes the repeated work between each approval gate. Both businesses stay connected while the material process moves from the contractor’s request to the supplier’s delivery.

1

Contractor completes the front-end work

The contractor builds the project scope, material takeoff and quantities once, inside CalibraytAI.

2

Contractor chooses the supplier and sends the request

The contractor selects the supplier account and sends the material request. The supplier sees only what the contractor sends.

3

Supplier quotes in the Supplier Hub

The supplier enters the product SKU, unit price and discount for each line on screen, or uploads the filled request. SKUs quoted before for the same product are filled in for checking; prices never are.

4

Contractor approves the quotation

The quotation lands on the contractor’s project, where the contractor records it and approves it.

5

Purchase order and confirmation

The contractor’s purchase order accepts the quotation. The supplier’s confirmation completes the agreement.

6

Delivery coordination

Delivery dates, delivery changes and their answers, and the delivered order stay connected to the project and show on the contractor’s calendar.

VALUE FOR SUPPLIERS

The supplier starts where the contractor’s work ends.

The supplier spends less time on takeoffs and order entry, and more on products, availability, fulfillment and service.

Receive the front-end work complete

The material request arrives organized by line, with quantities, units and project details already set out by the contractor.

Eliminate repeated takeoffs

Price the contractor’s requirement instead of recreating quantities from plans, notes and spreadsheets.

Quote from one screen

Enter SKUs, prices, discounts and how long the quotation is valid in the Supplier Hub, or upload the filled request.

Receive clear purchase orders

Orders follow from the quotation the contractor approved, so products, quantities and prices match what was quoted.

Coordinate deliveries

Confirm orders, answer delivery changes and mark orders delivered. Text alerts can tell staff when an order is placed or a delivery change is asked for.

See the demand

The Supplier Hub dashboard and material demand page show which contractors sent requests, what was asked for by product, what was priced and what was ordered.

VALUE FOR CONTRACTORS

Estimate faster and move directly toward delivery.

CalibraytAI keeps the project scope, material takeoff, supplier quotation, approved order and delivery schedule connected in one project workflow.

Save hours on every estimate

Build the material takeoff once and carry it through supplier pricing, project costing, ordering and delivery.

Use supplier quotations in the estimate

Bring the supplier’s quoted products and prices back into the project without separate calls, emails or spreadsheets.

Reduce missed materials

Keep scope, quantities, supplier products and estimate costing connected from the beginning.

Move directly into purchasing

Turn the approved quotation into a purchase order without rebuilding it in another system.

Control project cash flow

Organize purchases and deliveries around the work sequence instead of receiving the entire material package at once.

Keep the project moving

Agreed deliveries and delivery changes appear on the Company Calendar beside the work they support.

THE SUPPLIER HUB TODAY

What suppliers use in CalibraytAI now.

Supplier staff sign in to their own Supplier Hub. Each supplier sees its own account and the requests contractors have sent it, never another supplier’s work.

In the platform today

Supplier Hub

A separate sign-in for supplier staff, with requests, quotations, orders, deliveries and a dashboard for the supplier’s own account.

Contractor-authorized material requests

Deck material requests and concrete requests the contractor chooses to send. Nothing else from the contractor’s company is visible.

Supplier quotations

Prices, SKUs, discounts and valid-until dates entered by the supplier and returned to the contractor’s project.

Purchase orders and confirmations

The contractor’s purchase order and the supplier’s confirmation, recorded on both sides.

Delivery coordination

Delivery changes asked and answered, orders marked delivered, and text alerts for supplier staff.

Supplier catalogues and selling prices

The supplier loads its own price file of products and selling prices for use at the PRO Desk.

SUPPLIER PRO DESK

Walk-in quotations at the supplier’s counter.

The PRO Desk is for the supplier’s own walk-in customers. Those quotations belong to the supplier: contractors never see them, and nothing at the PRO Desk shows a contractor’s records.

In the platform today

Quick Quote and detailed quotations

Counter staff prepare a quotation for a walk-in customer, from a quick quote to a guided, line-by-line quotation.

Post-frame buildings first

Guided questions and a material takeoff for post-frame (pole barn) buildings with steel roof and wall cladding. Stick-framed and steel-frame buildings are not offered yet.

Priced from the supplier’s own price file

Lines are priced from the supplier’s loaded price file. A line without a price shows as needing one; nothing is guessed.

Quotation, drawing and order

Issue the quotation as a PDF with a drawing, record the customer’s acceptance and prepare the order.

INDEPENDENCE AND PRIVACY

The contractor’s business stays the contractor’s.

CalibraytAI connects contractors and suppliers without either one giving up control of its own commercial information.

1

Contractor independence

The contractor chooses which supplier receives each request and can work with more than one supplier.

2

Commercial privacy

Suppliers never see the contractor’s internal costs, margins, labour rates or other suppliers’ quotations.

3

Account portability

The contractor’s CalibraytAI company, projects and records belong to the contractor, whichever suppliers they buy from.

SUPPLIER-SPONSORED CONTRACTOR ACCESS

A concept we are developing with the industry.

We are exploring a model in which a supplier could sponsor CalibraytAI PRO accounts for the contractors it serves. It is a concept in development, not an offer: no supplier program or partnership has been agreed. Any program would keep the contractor’s account independent and portable, keep the contractor free to buy from any supplier, and keep the contractor’s commercial information private.
