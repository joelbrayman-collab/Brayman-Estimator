THE CONTRACTOR–SUPPLIER CONNECTION

Supplier Integration

The contractor does the project work once. CalibraytAI carries it through the entire supplier process.

CalibraytAI automatically transforms the contractor’s plans, takeoff and estimate into a complete supplier requirement. Products, costing, approval gates, ordering and scheduled deliveries remain connected—dramatically reducing the supplier’s takeoff and administrative workload.

THE WORK IS COMPLETED ONCE

The supplier receives the contractor’s front-end work—ready to process.

The contractor completes the plans, scope, takeoff, quantities, selections and estimate inside CalibraytAI. The platform automatically submits that complete material requirement to the supplier, where products, costs and availability are matched without rebuilding the project.

Contractor work completed

The project plan, takeoff and estimate become one complete supplier-ready material requirement.

Automatic supplier submission

CalibraytAI delivers the organized requirement directly into the supplier’s product and costing process.

Approval gates move it forward

Products, costing, ordering and delivery advance automatically as each project decision is approved.

AUTOMATED WITH APPROVAL GATES

Contractor work → Supplier costing → Approval → Order → Delivery

CalibraytAI automates the repetitive work between each approval gate. Both businesses stay connected while the material process moves from completed contractor requirements to scheduled supplier delivery.

1

Contractor completes the front-end work

CalibraytAI develops the project scope, material takeoff, quantities, selections and estimate once.

2

CalibraytAI submits the requirement

The complete plan, takeoff and material requirement are automatically delivered into the supplier connection.

3

Supplier products and costs are matched

The supplier’s catalogue, SKUs, contractor pricing and availability are automatically applied to the project.

4

Approval gate confirms the package

The contractor and supplier confirm products, quantities, costing and availability from the same connected information.

5

Estimate approval releases the order

CalibraytAI automatically converts the approved material package into a complete supplier order.

6

Approval gate coordinates delivery

Picking, loading, delivery groups and scheduling are organized around the contractor’s construction sequence.
