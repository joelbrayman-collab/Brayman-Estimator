IN THE PLATFORM TODAY

What a contractor runs in CalibraytAI now.

These are working parts of the contractor platform today, from the first drawing to the closed job.

In the platform today

Drawings and material takeoffs

Upload the plan PDFs to the project, review the permit report, and build material takeoffs for supported work such as decks and ICF foundation walls.

Construction calculators

Concrete, footings, stairs, ICF wall forms, wall framing, roofing, siding, drywall and flooring calculators, built into the platform.

Estimating, labour and material costing

Estimate versions with material, labour and subcontract lines. Scope review confirms who supplies and who installs before costing is approved, and your pricing policy sets the customer price.

Supplier selection and quotations

Choose the supplier, send the material request through the Supplier Hub, then record and approve the quotation that comes back.

Customer proposals and contracts

Create the customer proposal from the estimate, share it by private link, record acceptance and generate the construction contract from the accepted work.

Change orders and approvals

Record extra work and change orders against the project so scope, price and approvals stay with the job.

Company Calendar and My Calendar

The whole company’s work, crews, appointments and deliveries in one calendar, and each person’s own day, week and month.

Project scheduling and crew assignments

Schedule each piece of work, assign a crew or a person, and move a project’s dates together when the job shifts.

Personal and company appointments

Site visits, client meetings, supplier meetings and estimate consultations. Personal appointments are seen only by the person who made them.

Field schedules and time entry

Crews open today’s, this week’s and this month’s work on the phone, capture notes, photos and voice, and enter their time for the office to approve.

Project labour and cost performance

Compare the committed estimate with the actual direct costs entered for the project while the job is underway.

Purchasing, deliveries and closeout

Place purchase orders from approved quotations, follow confirmations and deliveries, answer delivery changes, and close the job with a punch list and final walkthrough. A QuickBooks-ready entry sheet is produced for the office.

Cal Assistant

Ask Cal to find a project, estimate, proposal, order, delivery or appointment, or how to do something. Cal only reads what you can already open; it never changes a record.

COMPANY CALENDAR AND MY CALENDAR

One connected place to organize jobs, people, appointments and deliveries.

The office sees the whole company. Each person sees their own day. The crew sees their work on the phone. Scheduled work, crew assignments, appointments and supplier deliveries all come from the same project records, so nobody keeps a second calendar to stay in step.

In the platform today

Company Calendar

Every scheduled job, crew assignment, company appointment and supplier delivery, in Day, Week or Month view. Filter to one project when you need to.

My Calendar

Your own work and appointments, including personal ones that no one else in the company can see.

Project scheduling and crew assignments

Put each piece of work on the calendar, assign the crew, and move a project’s dates together when the job shifts.

Appointments and site visits

Site visits, client meetings, supplier meetings and estimate consultations, linked to the client or project. Changes and cancellations are kept in the appointment’s history.

Delivery coordination

Deliveries agreed with the supplier, and delivery changes still waiting for an answer, show on the calendar beside the work they support.

Field schedule visibility

Crews see Today, This week and This month on the phone, with the job address and directions.

Your own calendar app

Subscribe from Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook to your private CalibraytAI address. It is a one-way feed: you make and change appointments in CalibraytAI, and they appear in your calendar app when that app refreshes. Any single appointment can also be downloaded as a calendar file.

The calendar feed is one-way and read-only. Nothing typed in Outlook, Google or Apple Calendar comes back into CalibraytAI, and CalibraytAI never asks for your Google, Microsoft or Apple password. Apple Calendar can refresh as often as every 5 minutes; Google and Outlook choose their own refresh time, usually within a few hours. The crew’s job schedule stays in Field rather than in the feed.
