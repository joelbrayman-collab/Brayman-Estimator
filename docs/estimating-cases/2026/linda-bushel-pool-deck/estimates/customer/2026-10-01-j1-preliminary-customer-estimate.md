## Scope

- Above-ground pool about 21 ft in diameter and about 50 in high. Field verify.
- Upper deck 18 ft wide. Pool edge curved and left open. Other three edges straight. 5 ft clear at the centreline.
- Option A framing: one straight main beam and two wing beams.
- 15 helical piers at the drawn locations. No sonotubes and no concrete. Shaft, helix, length, and capacity are not specified.
- 16 joist lines. 2×8 joists. Segmented curved header.
- Stairs 10 ft wide, about 38 in between surfaces, five risers about 7.6 in, four treads about 11 in. The upper deck is the top landing.
- 10 stair stringers. Each tread is two 5/4×6 boards.
- Working stringer throat 5.00 in. That figure is a design basis for this preliminary package. It is not an engineering certification.
- Lower deck 10 ft by 3 ft. Intended finished walking surface 12 in above grade.
- Guard: the named 37 in Veranda rail kit. Gate 42 in clear, outward swinging, self-closing and self-latching.
- 1×6 skirt on the lower deck only, installed tight.
- Freestanding. No structural attachment to the pool.

## Assumptions

- Brown pressure-treated lumber.
- Joist spacing is 16 in on centre maximum, with a 4 in closing space at each upper rim.
- Stringer spacing uses the same 4 in edge spaces. Ten stringers.
- Waste is the offcut from the stated stock lengths. No extra percentage is added.
- Lower 6×6 posts are ordered uncut. The post cut length is reconciled to the field before construction. The dressed beam, joist, and deck stack is 17.5 in.
- Pool-enclosure compliance, including the 1.2 m rule, remains subject to field and municipal verification. The 37 in kit is not redrawn to another height.
