CalibraytAI is one governed platform. Office pages are one interface. A later Field or public Website consumer must reuse the same business operation. This note does not change Architecture Principles Rules 1–12.

For a substantive business change:

1. Put the rule in a domain service, not in a template or route.
2. Store business state without depending on a desktop page, a phone page, or an HTML control.
3. Keep the human decision and its provenance on the record.
4. Leave plans and project files owned by the project.
5. Do not copy calculation mathematics into a page. Contract V1 remains the quantity handshake.

A future interface check, when the slice is substantive:

- Is the rule reusable?
- Is the stored state interface-neutral?
- Is the human decision durable?
- Is anything trapped in today’s desktop page?
- Would a later authenticated Field interface reuse this logic?

Do not add a public API, a PWA, or a native app merely because that check exists. A Desktop office / Field app / PWA readiness review is a later authorization.

Project Scope of work follows this note. `confirm_package` in `app/services/project_work_package.py` is the operation. `ProjectWorkPackage` is the record.
