## Plan Generation lessons from Bushel

Recorded only. The engine is not built.

- CT-1 shows that a framing plan has to draw each member, the piers, and the dimensions from one coordinate set.
- CT-2 R2 shows that a stair sheet is not finished when the outline exists. Rise, run, stringer length, throat, nosing, and the tread running under the riser have to be on the geometry.
- One measurement system belongs on the sheet. This Bushel set is imperial.
- A small undimensioned diagram is not a detail.
- Views and the take-off have to share one geometry. CT-1 and issue P1 do not. New sheets stop until that is one layout.
- Quantities come after the drawing is stable. They are not edited in silence.
