Professional control built into the work.

Confidence comes from a traceable record. Open the original source, inspect the quoted passage and follow the Bates reference back to the document. Matter and role permissions govern access; privilege boundaries remain part of the workflow. Attorney approvals, fixed report versions and activity history preserve who reviewed the work and what was approved.

Records control extends beyond the case. AiRIAdocs links retention decisions to documented rules, preserves legal holds and requires independent approval for disposition.

Review source traceability, access permissions, records controls and deployment requirements as part of your firm’s AiRIA evaluation.
