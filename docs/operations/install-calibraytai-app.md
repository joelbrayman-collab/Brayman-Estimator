# Installing CalibraytAI on a phone or computer

CalibraytAI can be added to a home screen like an app. It opens full screen with the gold CalibraytAI symbol. It is the same secure website: you still sign in, and signing out works the same way.

## iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1. Open the CalibraytAI address in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Leave the name as **CalibraytAI** and tap **Add**.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the CalibraytAI address in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**), then **Install**.

## Mac or Windows computer (Chrome or Edge)

1. Open the CalibraytAI address and sign in.
2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow), or use the menu → **Cast, save and share → Install page as app**.
3. Click **Install**.

## What is and is not stored on the device

- Office pages (projects, clients, estimates, proposals, costs) are never stored for offline use.
- The Field section keeps a copy of the field pages already opened by a signed-in field user, so the crew can see today's work with a weak signal. Signing out clears it.
- To remove the app, delete it from the home screen like any other app.
