# CalibraytAI — V1 User Manual

Brayman Construction. 7 October 2026.

This is the manual for the person running a Brayman job in the office Joel has opened. Help on each screen uses these same words. Help does not change the job.

## Home

You are on Home. It shows the work scheduled today, what needs attention, and the coming days. The month calendar lives on Company Calendar. This is not a report and not a score.

Read today, then open a job. Open Company Calendar when you need the month.

Open a current Project to plan, price, and run the job from the Project Hub.

If nothing is scheduled today, Home says so. If nothing needs attention, Home says so. Company attention stays with the people who run the company.

## Sign in

Open the address Joel gave you. Use the email and password Joel set. Choose Sign in.

There is no Create Account button. If you cannot sign in, ask Joel. Do not wait on a password email.

## Project

Open the project from Home or from Projects.

The project page is the hub. It holds the customer, the location, the estimate, the proposal, the contract status, the work, and the money for that job.

Print on the project is the same project name, number, and client already on the screen.

## Plan

Planning on the project is the Documents section.

Upload the plan PDF you already have. Review the job address. Read the permit report. A permit report is not municipal approval.

Calibrayt does not produce the crew drawing set.

An approved take-off package can be mapped into an estimate. Open an approved package and choose Map to estimate. Nothing is added until Insert into estimate.

## Price and costing

On the project, under Estimates and proposals, choose New estimate. Open the current version. Add the lines you know. Leave unknown lines off the estimate.

Print on the estimate lists the lines, quantities, and units already on the screen. It does not add a second estimate.

Open Scope Delivery Review. Confirm who provides the material and who does the work. Approve all costing requires that confirmation.

Confirm who provides the material and who does the work on Scope Delivery Review before Approve all costing.

An unconfirmed line blocks approval. Confirming the review does not approve the cost. If a line is still blocked, fix that line or take it off. Do not approve over a block.

Approve all costing approves the cost. It does not approve the customer price by itself, and it does not approve a supplier's price.

Then choose Apply org pricing policy. That button stays off until costing is approved.

## Customer estimate and proposal

On the estimate version, choose Create Proposal. Review it. The customer document title is CONSTRUCTION ESTIMATE. It is the price you are showing the customer. It is not the construction contract.

Print on the proposal is the same customer PDF. Read it before it leaves the office.

When the customer has committed, open that proposal. Set Update status to Accepted. Acceptance is recorded there, not on the project hub. An accepted proposal stays as it was. It is not the construction contract.

## Supplier Estimate Request

This sheet asks the supplier you selected for a price on this job. It is for this project only. It is not a standing price list.

Open the project, open Supplier Package, select the supplier, and download Supplier Estimate Request. The supplier fills in the product and the price. The supplier does not approve Brayman's cost.

When the sheet comes back, give it to Joel. Joel records the cost Brayman will use.

The path is Project, then Supplier Package, then select the supplier, then download Supplier Estimate Request.

Unit price and the supplier's product stay blank on the way out. A blank is not zero. Print on that screen downloads the same request.

Generate Supplier Package is a different record. It is not the supplier estimate request.

## QuickBooks

On the project, QuickBooks-ready entry is the sheet a person types into QuickBooks. Calibrayt produces the sheet. Calibrayt does not send the job to QuickBooks.

## Contract

Calibrayt uses the active Brayman V1 Ontario contract package. The contract package/version used for the project is recorded in the contract snapshot.

On the project, Contract shows Ontario, Brayman V1 Interim Contract, the version, the effective date, and the status. Open Review contract to generate the contract after the estimate is issued and locked and the proposal is issued or accepted.

Open the generated contract. It is the Brayman V1 interim contract. It has the parties, the project, the contract amount, and signature lines. Generating a contract does not sign it and does not send a signing link. A generated contract is not a signed contract.

If no active package is in force, or the project location cannot be resolved, or required contract data is missing, the contract stays blocked. Do not look for a way around that block.

This package is the Brayman V1 interim package. It is not marked counsel-approved. A later counsel-reviewed package can replace it. The snapshot for a project keeps the package that was used.

## Build, field, and change orders

On the project, Project work is where the job is run after the estimate exists.

On the phone or the office browser, open Field. Confirm the project. Capture the note, the photo, or the voice you actually have. Then confirm the note is listed under Field Observations.

Print on Field Today is the same scheduled work already on that screen.

A change order records what changed. On the project, choose New Change Order. Do not send the customer a Calibrayt signing link.

A field note is evidence. It is not an actual cost.

## Actuals and Money

Money is Estimated versus actual. It compares the committed estimate with actual direct costs entered in the office. It is not a profit figure.

Enter a labour, material, subcontract, or other direct cost only when you know the amount. Leave field notes as evidence. They do not fill the actual cost by themselves.

## Learn

Learn keeps what the job taught you. It does not recommend the next job and it does not change this one.

## Help and speaking

Each main screen has Help. Open it and read What, Do, and Next.

You can type a question. On a phone that can listen, you can choose Ask by speaking. On a phone that can speak, you can choose Speak answer.

The answer comes from Help. Speaking does not change the project, the estimate, or the schedule.

## Print

Print is on the screens a person would carry on paper: the project, the estimate, the proposal, the supplier estimate request, and Field Today.

The sheet uses the same facts already on the screen. Sign-in, password reset, and simple settings do not have Print.

## Backup

Joel owns backup and recovery. If the office will not open, stop and call Joel. Do not copy or rename the office file.

## When something goes wrong

Call Joel Brayman. Tell him the date, the project name, what you clicked, and the words on the screen.

Do not delete the project. Do not rewrite an accepted proposal. Do not type a contract.
