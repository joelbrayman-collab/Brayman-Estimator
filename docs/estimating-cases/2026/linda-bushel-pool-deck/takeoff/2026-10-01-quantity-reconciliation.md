## What does not depend on the open layout

These finished dimensions are the same in the design brief, issue P1, and the capability-test sheets:

- Pool diameter 21 ft. Radius 10.5 ft.
- Upper deck 18 ft wide. Clear depth at the centreline 5 ft.
- Stair 10 ft wide. Rise 38 in. Five risers at 7.60 in. Four treads at 11 in. Run 44 in.
- Lower deck 10 ft by 3 ft, recorded 12 in above grade.
- Gate 42 in clear, centred.
- Option A beam datum: main beam Y = 13 ft from X = −6 ft to +6 ft. Wing ends at X = ±9 ft, Y = 8.5 ft.

The dressed stack used on P1 sheet 1 is 1.00 + 7.25 + 9.25 = 17.50 in. The recorded lower-deck height is 12 in. The difference is 5.50 in. Lower post length stays unissued.
