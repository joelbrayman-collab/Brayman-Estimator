Assessment assumptions, methodology & references

Method: Owner-entered values are used in the calculation. Market benchmarks provide context and do not override owner assumptions. Typical cost defaults are planning estimates and can be replaced with actual figures.

Reference table: Ontario WSIB 2026 premium rates — calculation/planning context; Canada Revenue Agency 2026 CPP/EI — payroll context; Government of Canada Job Bank Ottawa-region trade wages — labour context; published Ottawa/Eastern Ontario contractor pricing guides — selling-rate context; published trades-business administration research — owner-time context.
